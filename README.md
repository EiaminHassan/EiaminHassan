## About

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate with an academic and research-oriented
interest in **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, **Computer Vision**, and
**Large Language Models (LLMs)**. My work focuses on turning computational methods into
reproducible, practical systems, with particular interests in LLM research, image
understanding, and medical image analysis.

I am currently conducting research on **LLMs**, while also exploring how intelligent
systems can support meaningful analysis of complex visual and medical data. I enjoy
combining mathematical foundations, software engineering, and empirical experimentation
to investigate problems, communicate results clearly, and build reliable AI applications.

## Research interests

- Computer vision and image processing
- Medical image analysis
- Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative AI
- Machine learning and deep learning
- End-to-end AI system development
